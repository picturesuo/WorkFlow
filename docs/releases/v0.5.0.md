# WorkFlow 0.5.0

WorkFlow now adapts cleanup to what you are writing without pretending every dictation should be concise.

## Writing modes

- **Homework** preserves every stated idea and develops compressed reasoning into complete prose. Longer output is intentional.
- **Technical** produces concise, unambiguous instructions for computers and coding agents while retaining constraints, paths, flags, identifiers, and acceptance criteria.
- **Everyday** removes speech artifacts while preserving natural voice and detail.

Choose a mode in the main WorkFlow window or directly from its menu-bar menu before holding `Fn`.

## Token-efficiency statistics

Every successful cleanup now records a local estimate of source and final tokens. History shows the result for each dictation, and **Settings → Cleanup** reports each mode's normalized monthly efficiency plus direct Technical-versus-Homework and Technical-versus-Everyday comparisons.

The comparison is provider-independent and computed locally as source tokens ÷ final tokens using a geometric mean across dictations. It is deliberately labeled an estimate. Provider-reported billing tokens and costs remain separate, and failed or budget-limited cleanup attempts are excluded.

## Safety and privacy

- The shared prompt forbids invented facts, numbers, code, citations, and answers.
- A local guard rejects provider output containing numeric values absent from the source.
- Only the selected mode and numeric estimates are stored for efficiency; WorkFlow does not keep a second pre-cleanup transcript copy.
- Legacy History remains intact and is excluded from the new comparison when source metrics are unavailable.
- Audio and local token estimates remain on the Mac.
